# Qinfeng Tuberculosis Olink Data

Wide-format Olink NPX data from a tuberculosis biomarker discovery study
by Song et al. (2025). Contains 92 protein measurements for 66 subjects.

## Usage

``` r
qinfeng_npx
```

## Format

A data.table with 66 rows and 97 columns, including SampleID, 92 protein
NPX columns, and QC metrics.

## Source

Song Y, Abuduaini B, Yang X, Zhang J, Wang G, Lu X (2025).
Identification of inflammatory protein biomarkers for predicting the
different subtype of adult with tuberculosis: an Olink proteomic study.
Inflammation Research.
[doi:10.1007/s00011-025-02020-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00011-025-02020-9)
