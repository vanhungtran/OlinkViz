# BeatMG Demographics

Demographic and clinical annotation data for the BeatMG Myasthenia
Gravis study. One row per subject.

## Usage

``` r
beatmg_demo
```

## Format

A data.frame with 52 rows and 42 columns:

- SubjectID:

  Subject identifier (links to beatmg_npx)

- Age:

  Age of subject

- Sex:

  Biological sex

- Ethnicity:

  Self-reported ethnicity

- ...:

  Additional clinical and demographic variables

## Source

BeatMG study (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02110706). Nowak RJ, et al. (2022).
Phase 2 Trial of Rituximab in Acetylcholine Receptor Antibody-Positive
Generalized Myasthenia Gravis: The BeatMG Study. Neurology.
[doi:10.1212/WNL.0000000000013121](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000013121)
